# 2026 2B1 Observation Cycle

## Purpose

Use the completed NIT3004 teaching block to understand how students actually use
Check-in, Intake, Activities and Work Track, and to turn those findings into an
evidence-based Sprint 8 plan.

Sprint 7 is the observation and feedback window. With the exception of a
separately reviewed Landing Page visual improvement, it should avoid changing
the active learning workflow during the final two Sessions.

## Cohort baseline

- Starting cohort: **17 students**
- Withdrawn during the block: **3 students**
- Current active cohort: **14 students**
- Teams represented in the current roster: **6**

The three withdrawn students are not missing submissions and must be excluded
from active participation denominators. Historical figures showing 17 students
should be labelled as starting-cohort figures rather than treated as current
Dashboard defects.

### Stage 2 — Final teaching-block review

Status: pending completion of the final Monday Session.

Add:

- final S9 and S10 participation
- S10 Platform Feedback
- Poster participation
- Teacher Review and verification
- final submission/readiness evidence available to the platform
- unresolved support or follow-up state
- confirmed active/withdrawn denominator

The final review should trace:

```text
Intake
  → participation
  → progress evidence
  → teacher verification/intervention
  → final readiness/outcome
```

### Stage 3 — Sprint 8 evidence decisions

After Stage 2, record each proposed change under `decisions/` using:

- observed problem
- supporting evidence
- student benefit
- teacher intervention benefit
- smallest viable change
- success measure for the next block
- whether deterministic logic is sufficient
- whether AI has a justified, bounded role
