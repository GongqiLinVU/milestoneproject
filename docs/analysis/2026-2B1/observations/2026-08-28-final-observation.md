## Evidence interpretation

Classroom-timed checkpoints were substantially more reliable than unsupported
self-directed forms. High form completion did not by itself imply useful
evidence.

Broad Team Health answers were predominantly Clear, Balanced and On track, yet
final Teacher Review found only 2/13 fully verified outcomes. Eight were
Partially verified, two Unable to demonstrate and one required further evidence.
Eight of thirteen had only Partial evidence, and five required further evidence
for individual contribution.

Among S9 submitters, average student-reported completion was 93.4%; six reported
100%. Several of those students nevertheless remained Partially verified,
Unable to demonstrate or in need of further evidence. Completion must therefore
always be presented with coverage, evidence and Teacher verification.

Teacher Review produced the most actionable evidence: missing demonstration,
open-source contribution boundaries, duplicate or shallow responsibility,
integration gaps, insufficient testing and limited technical explanation.

## Final Report evaluation

Five project cases confirmed that report polish, breadth and test counts do not
reliably predict quality.

Decisive assessment cases:

- unsupported breadth and headline test counts without proportional evidence
- structured workflow evidence without deep technical validation
- original implementation with almost no executed experiment/testing
- honest model failure, measurement, iteration and retest as positive evidence
- successful infrastructure deployment without the project's promised data
  analysis and stakeholder decision outcome

Team Report evaluation establishes a shared baseline. Individual results require
a second layer using identifiable contribution, progress evidence, live
explanation, Teacher observations, leadership, assistance and approved
supplementary work.

## Product conclusion

> Report is a claim; Activity is a process record; Artifact is work evidence;
> Teacher Review is verification; AI connects them.

A Report-per-Session model should not require a long student-authored document.
A short adaptive AI evidence interview should produce the structured Session
Report, compare it with prior evidence and prepare Teacher verification
questions.
