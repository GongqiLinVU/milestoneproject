# Sprint 8 Evidence Decisions

## Accepted direction

### Pilot — AI Session Intake

Replace repetitive fixed progress forms with a bounded, adaptive evidence
interview. Keep a stable structured schema and generate the Session Report after
student confirmation.

**Evidence:** low sustained form participation; idealised broad answers; much
higher value from specific implementation and Teacher verification evidence.

### Pilot — Teacher Verification Loop

Connect AI-generated questions, Teacher verification, required action, student
response and recheck.

**Evidence:** Teacher Review exposed decisive issues not visible in polished
reports or self-reported completion.

### Refine — Work Track

Retain requirement progression, but separate Student-reported progress, Evidence
available and Teacher verified status. Merge the useful Work Track purpose into
AI Session Intake rather than adding another parallel form.

### Refine — Intake

Replace broad questions with Responsibility, Deliverable, Evidence, Dependency,
Next action and Due Session. Absorb Four-Week Promise and useful Team Health
signals.

### Retain — Session Check-in

Attendance remains separate from work evidence. It is operationally useful and
was selected by students as a valuable feature.

### Retain — Teacher Review

Teacher verification remains the academic and evidence boundary. AI prepares
the review; it does not make the decision.

### Retain and refine — Poster / Peer Feedback

All Teams published Posters and feedback aligned with Teacher-observed
weaknesses, particularly testing, product flow and documentation. Future design
should require a bounded number of reviews and a response to one received
priority.

### Remove from active journey — Four-Week Promise

Only 5/14 completed it and its purpose overlaps with responsibility and evidence
planning.

### Investigate — Reminders

Students requested incomplete-activity notifications, but reminders should be
considered only after the Session experience is simplified.

### Bounded pilot — Final Report Evaluation

Use the five project cases as labelled evidence. AI extracts claims, maps
evidence, identifies missing proof and generates targeted questions. Teacher
owns rubric interpretation, individual adjustment and final marks.

## Success measures for the next block

- higher Intake completion than the equivalent fixed-form Work Track
- median normal completion within five minutes
- most records contain specific responsibility, evidence and next action
- fewer unsupported 100% readiness claims
- shorter Teacher review time without lower verification quality
- useful suggested verification questions with low false-flag rate
- unresolved Teacher actions visibly carried to the next Session
